# Unresolved Questions

## Purpose

This file preserves questions that have emerged during Bible study but have not yet been settled by the biblical text and the work completed so far. An open question is part of an honest study record, not a defect that must be hidden.

## Governing safeguards

- Do not force a conclusion to make the record look complete.
- Do not confuse absence of an answer in the current study with proof that no answer exists.
- Keep the wording of Scripture distinct from inference and interpretation.
- Record relevant passages on more than one side of a question.
- Do not judge a person's heart, faith, or spiritual state from disagreement, uncertainty, or limited comprehension.
- Do not alter GUA-W to make an unresolved question disappear.
- If a question requires scientific investigation, keep it open while conducting or scheduling a properly sourced investigation within this unified repository. Do not mark it outside scope merely because it is scientific.
