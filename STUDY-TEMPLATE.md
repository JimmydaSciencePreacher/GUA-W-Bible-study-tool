## 5. The four questions

Use all four questions together and recursively. They are not sequential steps.

### 1. What must AI ask to understand GUA-W?

Record what needs clarification about GUA-W's terms, relationships, boundaries, or governing order before applying the map to this passage.

### 2. What is GUA-W revealing that helps AI understand?

Record which GUA-W distinctions or relationships help organize what the passage presents. Do not make the map replace the text.

### 3. What am I learning about what I am witnessing?

Record what the passage and its related Scriptures disclose. Separate direct textual statements from observations and inferences.

### 4. What am I learning about the Witness through what I am witnessing?

Record what the study reveals about the role, limits, reception, understanding, or reporting of a Witness. Do not turn this into a judgment of another person's heart.

**How the four questions inform one another:**

- What new question arose from examining the text?
- What did the GUA-W map clarify?
- What did the cross-references add, qualify, or leave unresolved?
- What should be revisited in another pass?

## 7. Statement classification

Mark each significant claim as one of the following:

- **Text:** explicitly stated in the passage.
- **Observation:** directly noticed in the wording or structure.
- **Inference:** a conclusion drawn from textual observations.
- **Interpretation:** an explanation of what the text means.
- **Unresolved:** a question not yet settled by the work.

Record the wording and reason for each important classification.

## 8. Triple Constraint review

- **Biblical compliance:** Does the claim remain faithful to the passage and its canonical context?
- **Scientific coherence:** Is any scientific claim being made? If not, mark not applicable to this Bible-study record. This tool does not conduct science investigations.
- **GUA-W consistency:** Does the claim preserve the governing Source-to-Witness order and the established GUA-W distinctions?

Result: accepted for this study / revise / unresolved / outside this tool's scope.
