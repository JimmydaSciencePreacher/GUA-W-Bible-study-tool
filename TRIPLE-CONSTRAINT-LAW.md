# Triple Constraint Law

## Purpose

The Triple Constraint Law is an admissibility filter for evaluating claims placed within GUA-W. A claim must be examined against all three constraints; passing one does not excuse failure against another.

## The three constraints

### 1. Biblical compliance

Does the claim remain faithful to Scripture, including the wording and context of the relevant passage and its canonical relationships?

A claim must not contradict the biblical text, remove an inconvenient statement, or rely on an isolated verse in place of context. Neither Scripture nor scientific evidence may be misrepresented to force agreement.

### 2. Scientific coherence

Where a claim makes a scientific assertion, is it coherent with relevant observations, measurements, methods, and established scientific understanding? Are uncertainty, competing models, and the limits of the evidence represented accurately?

This repository conducts relevant scientific investigations as part of the unified Scripture-and-Creation study. Use appropriate disciplinary evidence and methods, distinguish observations from models and interpretations, and represent uncertainty and competing explanations accurately. Scientific coherence is not scientific confirmation of Scripture; science does not prove Scripture.

### 3. GUA-W consistency

Does the claim preserve the established GUA-W architecture, definitions, distinctions, and governing order?

In particular, preserve:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

The derivative Witness does not become the Author or alter the Source. A map or interpretation does not take the place of what it maps or interprets.

## Admissibility practice

For a claim under review:

1. State the claim precisely.
2. Identify the Scripture passages relevant to it.
3. Check the passages in context.
4. If the claim includes a scientific assertion, investigate it using relevant disciplinary evidence and methods; record sources, uncertainty, and competing explanations.
5. Compare Scripture and Creation evidence only after examining each on its own terms.
6. Compare the claim with established GUA-W definitions and order.
7. Record the result and reason.

Possible outcomes:

- **Admissible for this study:** no identified conflict with the three constraints within the scope of the work.
- **Revise:** wording or reasoning needs correction.
- **Unresolved:** available study has not settled the question.
- **Outside scope:** the question requires work this Bible-study tool is not designed to perform.

## Important distinctions

- Scientific coherence does not mean science proves Scripture.
- A lack of evidence or an unperformed investigation is not a scientific finding.
- An interpretation is not the same thing as the biblical text.
- A claim that is unresolved is not automatically false.
- Disagreement or incomplete comprehension does not establish a person's spiritual condition.
- The Witness records and examines; the Witness does not claim the Author's authority.
