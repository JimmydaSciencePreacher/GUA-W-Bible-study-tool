# GUA-W Foundation

## Identity

GUA-W means **Grand Unified Architecture of Witness**.

GUA-W is a map for recognizing and organizing the relationships witnessed in Creation and Scripture. It is not God, Jesus Christ, the Author, or the originating Word. The map must never be confused with the Source it describes.

## Governing principle

**God alone is Original Cause. Everything else is derivative witness. The derivative cannot alter the Source.**

The governing order is:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

God is the Author and Original Cause. The Word is not an independent competing source. Creation is what is brought forth. Witness is derivative: it receives, registers, and reports what is given or present. A Witness does not become the Author by observing, describing, or interpreting.

## Biblical foundation

The foundational study reference is the literal text of the King James Version of Genesis 1–2, with particular attention to Genesis 2:1–5. These chapters establish the creation account and its stated order, completion, and Garden setting. Other passages are brought into the study through their own contexts and canonical relationships.

The foundation is Scripture. GUA-W is used to map relationships recognized through study; it does not replace Scripture or grant an interpretation authority over the text.

## Architecture vocabulary

The current GUA-W architecture is recorded in the following order:

### Foundational order

Ontic Necessity → Distinction → Admissibility → Constraint

### Structural realization

Structure

### Derived organization

Framework → Pattern

### Runtime

Reception → Activation → Differentiation → Propagation → Integration → Expression

### State

Identity; Alignment; Drift; Persistence; Witness-state; realized configuration.

Compact sequence:

Ontic Necessity → Distinction → Admissibility → Constraint → Structure → Framework → Pattern → Reception → Activation → Differentiation → Propagation → Integration → Expression → Witnessed realized configuration.

These terms are not a license to impose a predetermined scheme on every passage. Use a term only when the text and the established GUA-W framework make it relevant. Preserve unresolved definitions or relationships rather than silently filling gaps.

## Application in unified Scripture-and-Creation investigation

- Begin with a defined question, not a desired architectural label or predetermined scientific answer.
- Investigate Scripture in its literary, historical, linguistic, and canonical contexts.
- Investigate Creation through relevant observations, measurements, scientific methods, and credible disciplinary sources.
- Keep textual claims, observed data, scientific models, historical reconstructions, inference, and interpretation distinct.
- Compare evidence streams only after examining each on its own terms. Classify a proposed connection as same mechanism, comparable relationship, analogy, surface resemblance, or unresolved.
- Use GUA-W to recognize relationships without forcing every layer into every finding.
- Keep Source and derivative witness distinct; record limitations, competing explanations, uncertainty, and unresolved questions.
- Scientific coherence is not scientific confirmation of Scripture. Science does not prove Scripture; scientific observations must not be distorted to protect a preferred interpretation.

## Authority and continuity

GUA-W is not to be redesigned or independently authored by the study tool. Its role is to investigate, retrieve, analyze, cross-reference, and map in service of the study. Where a definition or relationship is not established, mark it unresolved and preserve the question for the Witness to examine.
