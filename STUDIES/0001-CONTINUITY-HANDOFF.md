# Study 0001 — Continuity Handoff

## Current position
Genesis 1:1–3 (KJV) has been read in sequence.

- Genesis 1:1 — God created the heaven and the earth.
- Genesis 1:2 — the earth's stated condition; darkness upon the face of the deep; the Spirit of God moving upon the face of the waters.
- Genesis 1:3 — God said, “Let there be light”; and there was light.

## Governing starting point
The Author creates. The architecture becomes recognizable through the Author's work as the account unfolds. The Witness observes and maps what is revealed. The map follows the Author's work; it does not prescribe it.

## Continuity rules

- Do not import previous study conclusions or GitHub history.
- Do not impose a completed architecture on the text.
- Distinguish what the verse says from observation, inference, interpretation, and unresolved questions.
- Do not run ahead or assign a function before the text supports it.
