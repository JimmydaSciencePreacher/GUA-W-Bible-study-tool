# Study 0001 — Continuity Handoff

## Current position
Genesis 1:1–6 (KJV) has been examined in the active study.

- Genesis 1:1 — God created the heaven and the earth.
- Genesis 1:2 — the earth's described condition; darkness upon the face of the deep; the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.
- Genesis 1:3 — God said, “Let there be light”; and there was light.
- Genesis 1:4 — God saw the light, that it was good; and God divided the light from the darkness.
- Genesis 1:5 — God called the light Day and the darkness Night; the evening and the morning were the first day.
- Genesis 1:6 — God said, “Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters,” and that it divide the waters from the waters. The verse records the command; the result is in the next verse.

## Governing starting point
The Author creates. The architecture becomes recognizable through the Author's work as the account unfolds. The Witness observes and maps what is revealed. The map follows the Author's work; it does not prescribe it.

This is a guiding proposition, not a completed architecture to impose upon the verses.

## Continuity safeguards
- Preserve the exact KJV wording and the order of the text.
- Distinguish God's observation in Genesis 1:4 from the Witness's reading.
- Keep division (Genesis 1:4) distinct from naming (Genesis 1:5).
- Distinguish the command in Genesis 1:6 from its result in Genesis 1:7.
- Do not assume a physical mechanism or force a fixed architectural label.
- GitHub history may be used to recover handoff continuity only; it is not a source of Scripture interpretation or study conclusions.
