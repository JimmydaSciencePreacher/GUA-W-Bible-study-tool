# Study 0001 — Continuity Handoff

**Current position:** Genesis 1:1–16 (KJV).

**Next passage:** Genesis 1:17 (KJV).

## Sequence recorded so far
- Genesis 1:1 — God created the heaven and the earth.
- Genesis 1:2 — the earth's described condition; darkness upon the face of the deep; the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.
- Genesis 1:3 — God said, “Let there be light”; and there was light.
- Genesis 1:4 — God saw the light, that it was good; and God divided the light from the darkness.
- Genesis 1:5 — God called the light Day and the darkness Night; the evening and the morning were the first day.
- Genesis 1:6 — God commanded that there be a firmament in the midst of the waters and that it divide the waters from the waters.
- Genesis 1:7 — God made the firmament, divided the waters under it from the waters above it, and the text says, “and it was so.”
- Genesis 1:8 — God called the firmament Heaven; the evening and the morning were the second day. The verse does not record “and God saw that it was good.”
- Genesis 1:9 — God commanded the waters under heaven to be gathered unto one place and the dry land to appear; the text says, “and it was so.”
- Genesis 1:10 — God called the dry land Earth and the gathering together of the waters Seas; God saw that it was good.
- Genesis 1:11 — God commanded the earth to bring forth grass, the herb yielding seed, and the fruit tree yielding fruit after his kind, whose seed is in itself; the text says, “and it was so.”
- Genesis 1:12 — The earth brought forth grass, herb yielding seed after his kind, and the tree yielding fruit, whose seed was in itself, after his kind; God saw that it was good.
- Genesis 1:13 — The evening and the morning were the third day.
- Genesis 1:14 — God commanded lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide day from night and to be for signs, seasons, days, and years.
- Genesis 1:15 — The command continues: the lights are to give light upon the earth; the text says, “and it was so.”
- Genesis 1:16 — God made two great lights: the greater to rule the day, the lesser to rule the night; He made the stars also.
