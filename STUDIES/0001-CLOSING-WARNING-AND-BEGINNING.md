## Genesis 1:4 (KJV)

> And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness.

### Text and observations

The verse records:
1. God saw the light.
2. God saw that it was good.
3. God divided the light from the darkness.

The grammatical subject of “saw” is God. The declaration “that it was good” belongs to what the verse says God saw. The division is an action attributed to God.

### What we are witnessing

Genesis 1:4 adds to the recorded sequence. After the command and the stated presence of light in verse 3, this verse records God's seeing, His assessment that the light was good, and His dividing the light from the darkness.

Keep the distinction clear:
- **Author's observation:** God saw the light, that it was good.
- **Author's action:** God divided the light from the darkness.
- **Our witness:** We read and record what the text says God saw and did. We do not replace God's observation with our own evaluation.
