**Revelation 22:18**

> For I testify unto every man that heareth the words of the prophecy of this book, If any man shall add unto these things, God shall add unto him the plagues that are written in this book:

**Revelation 22:19**

> And if any man shall take away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God shall take away his part out of the book of life, and out of the holy city, and from the things which are written in this book.

**Revelation 22:20**

> He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.

**Revelation 22:21**

> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.

## 3. First observations — before interpretation

- Verse 18 begins, “For I testify,” and identifies the audience as every man that hears the words of the prophecy of “this book.”
- Verse 18 states a warning concerning adding to “these things.”
- Verse 19 states a corresponding warning concerning taking away from the words of “the book of this prophecy.”
- Verse 20 identifies the One who testifies these things and records the declaration, “Surely I come quickly,” followed by “Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.”
- Verse 21 closes with a statement of grace and “Amen.”

These are initial observations of the wording. Their full scope and relationship to the preceding chapter and the book as a whole require examination.
