## 1. Why begin here?

The study begins at the closing verses of the Bible, Revelation 22:18–19, before moving to the opening verse, Genesis 1:1. This establishes the study's first discipline: receive the written text carefully; do not add to it, take from it, or make an interpretation equal to the text.
