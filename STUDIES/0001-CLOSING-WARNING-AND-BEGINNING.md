## Genesis 1:3 (KJV)

> And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.

### Text

The verse records two clauses in this order:

1. “And God said, Let there be light:”
2. “and there was light.”

### Observations

- God is the subject of the stated action “said.”
- The words spoken are “Let there be light.”
- The text then states, “and there was light.”
- The verse presents God's speaking before the stated existence of light.
- The verse does not identify the physical source, composition, or properties of the light.
- The verse does not yet say that God divided the light from the darkness or named either one. Those statements occur in the next verse and must remain in their textual order.

### Inference

The narrative places God's spoken command and the existence of light in direct sequence. This is a textual sequence; the study should not add an unstated mechanism or details about how the light came to be.
