# GUA-W Bible Study Tool

## Purpose

The GUA-W Bible Study Tool uses the Grand Unified Architecture of Witness (GUA-W) as its framework for studying Scripture. Its purpose is to help a reader examine the biblical text, follow Scripture's own cross-references, recognize relationships, and record what is established, what is inferred, and what remains unresolved.

This repository conducts a unified Scripture-and-Creation investigation. Scripture is examined in biblical context; Creation is investigated through relevant observations and the methods and evidence of the sciences. The evidence streams are compared without forcing one to say what the other has not established.

## Governing reference

The foundation is the King James Version (KJV) of Genesis 1–2, with particular attention to Genesis 2:1–5. The governing order is:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

God alone is Original Cause. Everything else is derivative witness. The derivative does not alter the Source. GUA-W is a map for recognizing and organizing what is witnessed; it is not the Author, the Word, God, or Jesus Christ.

# GUA-W-Language-of-Creation

Rather than having every scientific discipline dictate its own vocabulary for describing Creation, we establish a common descriptive language and investigate whether that language adequately accounts for what science observes.

This is a new scientific research sequence beginning at Investigation 001. Scientific terminology, evidence, methods, mechanisms, uncertainty, and competing explanations remain alongside every GUA-W description.

The common language is an **investigative grammar for comparison, not a claim of identical mechanisms**. It helps identify and compare components, relationships, constraints, operations, manifestations, and witnesses across domains. Each discipline retains its own established terminology and causal account. A connection must be classified as the same mechanism, a comparable relationship, an analogy, a surface resemblance, or unresolved. The vocabulary must clarify the evidence; it must never force the evidence into predetermined categories.

## The four questions

These four questions are used together and recursively throughout a study:

1. **What must AI ask to understand GUA-W?**
2. **What is GUA-W revealing that helps AI understand?**
3. **What am I learning about what I am witnessing?**
4. **What am I learning about the Witness through what I am witnessing?**

They are not four sequential stages. Each question can inform and refine the others as Scripture and Creation are investigated.

## Scope and boundaries

- Scripture is examined in its immediate, book, and canonical contexts.
- KJV wording is preserved when the KJV is the stated study text.
- Cross-references are investigated rather than treated as automatic proof of an interpretation.
- GUA-W is recognized and applied as the governing map; this tool does not redesign or independently author GUA-W.
- Distinguish what the text says from observation, inference, interpretation, and unresolved questions.
- Do not judge a person's heart or assign spiritual state from disagreement, uncertainty, or incomplete comprehension.
- Creation is investigated through relevant observations, measurements, scientific methods, and disciplinary sources.
- Distinguish textual claims, observations, scientific models, inferences, interpretations, and unresolved questions.
- Compare evidence streams only after examining each on its own terms; classify a connection as same mechanism, comparable relationship, analogy, surface resemblance, or unresolved.
- The Triple Constraint Law applies to claims proposed for GUA-W placement: biblical compliance, scientific coherence, and GUA-W consistency. Scientific coherence is not scientific confirmation; science does not prove Scripture.

## Relationship to The New Witness

The New Witness is a template reference for repository organization, study discipline, and reusable methods. This repository does not import its investigation data, scientific subject matter, findings, or conclusions.
