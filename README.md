# GUA-W Bible Study Tool

## Purpose

The GUA-W Bible Study Tool uses the Grand Unified Architecture of Witness (GUA-W) as its framework for studying Scripture. Its purpose is to help a reader examine the biblical text, follow Scripture's own cross-references, recognize relationships, and record what is established, what is inferred, and what remains unresolved.

This repository studies the Bible. It is not a science-study repository.

## Governing reference

The foundation is the King James Version (KJV) of Genesis 1–2, with particular attention to Genesis 2:1–5. The governing order is:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

God alone is Original Cause. Everything else is derivative witness. The derivative does not alter the Source. GUA-W is a map for recognizing and organizing what is witnessed; it is not the Author, the Word, God, or Jesus Christ.

## The four questions

These four questions are used together and recursively throughout a study:

1. **What must AI ask to understand GUA-W?**
2. **What is GUA-W revealing that helps AI understand?**
3. **What am I learning about what I am witnessing?**
4. **What am I learning about the Witness through what I am witnessing?**

They are not four sequential stages. Each question can inform and refine the others as Scripture is examined.

## Scope and boundaries

- Scripture is examined in its immediate, book, and canonical contexts.
- KJV wording is preserved when the KJV is the stated study text.
- Cross-references are investigated rather than treated as automatic proof of an interpretation.
- GUA-W is recognized and applied as the governing map; this tool does not redesign or independently author GUA-W.
- Distinguish what the text says from observation, inference, interpretation, and unresolved questions.
- Do not judge a person's heart or assign spiritual state from disagreement, uncertainty, or incomplete comprehension.
- The Triple Constraint Law is retained as a GUA-W admissibility principle. This repository does not conduct scientific investigations, create a science track, or claim that science proves Scripture.

## Relationship to The New Witness

The New Witness is a template reference for repository organization, study discipline, and reusable methods. This repository does not import its investigation data, scientific subject matter, findings, or conclusions.

## Continuity

Record the passage, questions, cross-references, findings, and unresolved matters so that another study can continue without changing what has already been established.
